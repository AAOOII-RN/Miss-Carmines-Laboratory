# Miss Carmine's Laboratory
**This game features implicit gore that may be unsuitable for those who are easily disturbed**

## About the story
A visual novel about love, obsession, and the cost of refusing to let go.

Miss Carmine devoted her life to reviving the one she lost — pouring years of failure, grief, and desperate science into a single miracle: #1430. He wakes with no memory of dying, only the quiet ache of a name he isn't called anymore, and a body that was never truly meant to last.

As his "fixes" spiral into something crueler than the cure, #1430 must confront a question neither science nor love can answer: is survival worth the price of endless pain — and how far will the person who loves him go to keep him from choosing otherwise?

*A story about grief disguised as devotion, and the different ways two people might finally find peace.*
